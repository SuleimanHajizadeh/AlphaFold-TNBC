# Source - Structural Bioinformatics Course Curriculum

* **Location**: `Structural Biology Tutorials/Structural-Bioinformatics/`
* **Author / Institution**: Carlo Camilloni, Department of Biosciences, University of Milano
* **Focus**: Advanced computational methods for modeling, simulating, and designing biomolecular structures and dynamics.

---

## 📌 Core Curriculum Structure

1. **Structural Biology & Visualization**: Experimental methods (X-ray, Cryo-EM, NMR) and molecular graphics (VMD, PyMOL).
2. **Statistical Mechanics of Biomolecules**: Stochastic dynamics, energy landscapes, Boltzmann distributions, and free energy profiles.
3. **Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulations**:
   * Classical mechanics integrators (Verlet, Leapfrog).
   * Empirical force fields (AMBER, CHARMM, GROMOS).
   * Solvent models (explicit TIP3P/TIP4P vs implicit Generalized Born).
4. **Enhanced Sampling Techniques**: Metadynamics, Replica Exchange MD (REMD), and coarse-grained modeling (Martini).
5. **Markov State Models (MSMs)**: Discretization of conformational space, transition probability matrices, and kinetic rate constants.
6. **Structure Prediction & Molecular Docking**: Template-based modeling, deep learning architectures ([[AlphaFold2]]), rigid/flexible receptor docking, scoring functions.
7. **QM/MM & Hybrid Simulations**: Density Functional Theory (DFT) coupled with classical MM for enzyme catalysis.
8. **Integrative Modeling & De Novo Protein Design**: Hallucination, diffusion models (RFdiffusion), and inverse folding (ProteinMPNN).
