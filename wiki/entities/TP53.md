# TP53 (Tumor Protein p53)

**TP53** is the principal human tumor suppressor gene, frequently termed the "Guardian of the Genome". It encodes the transcription factor p53, which responds to cellular stress (DNA damage, hypoxia, oncogene activation) by triggering cell cycle arrest, DNA repair, senescence, or apoptosis.

---

## 🧬 Genomic & Structural Coordinates

* **Canonical mRNA Accession**: NCBI RefSeq [NM_000546.6](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/NM_000546.6) (2,629 bp).
* **Genomic Locus**: Chromosome 17p13.1 ([NC_000017.11:7,668,402–7,687,538](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/NC_000017.11), 19.1 kb).
* **Domain Architecture**:
  * N-terminal transactivation domain (TAD)
  * Proline-rich domain (PRD)
  * Central core DNA-binding domain (DBD) — site of >80% of cancer mutations
  * Oligomerization domain (OD)
  * C-terminal regulatory domain (CTD)

## ⚠️ Mutational Hotspots in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)

In TNBC and broader oncology, TP53 missense mutations disrupt sequence-specific DNA binding:
* **Codon 175** (e.g., R175H): Structural mutation distorting zinc coordination in the core domain.
* **Codon 248** (e.g., R248W/Q): Direct DNA-contact mutation at the minor groove.
* **Codon 273** (e.g., R273H/C): Direct DNA-contact residue at the phosphate backbone.
