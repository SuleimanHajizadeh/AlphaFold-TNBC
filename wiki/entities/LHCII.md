# LHCII (Light-Harvesting Complex II)

**Light-Harvesting Complex II (LHCII)** is the major light-harvesting antenna complex of Photosystem II (PSII) in photosynthetic eukaryotes (plants and green algae). It is predominantly organized as membrane-embedded trimers composed of apoproteins encoded by the *Lhcb1*, *Lhcb2*, and *Lhcb3* genes, binding chlorophyll $a$, chlorophyll $b$, and carotenoids (lutein, neoxanthin, violaxanthin).

---

## ⚡ Role in Thylakoid State Transitions

```
PSII (Appressed Grana) ──[ Unphosphorylated LHCII ]──► High light capture for PSII
                                  │
                       STN7 Kinase Phosphorylation
                                  │
                                  ▼
PSI (Stroma Lamellae)  ◄──[ Phosphorylated P-LHCII ]── Detaches & docks to PSI (State 2)
```

1. **Substrate of [[STN7]]**: Under conditions where PSII is over-excited relative to PSI, [[STN7]] phosphorylates the N-terminal threonine residues of **Lhcb1** and **Lhcb2** (specifically Thr-3 in mature Lhcb1/2).
2. **Charge-Driven Translocation**: Phosphorylation introduces negative charges that destabilize the electrostatic interactions holding LHCII trimers within the appressed grana membranes, enabling lateral diffusion into the unappressed stroma lamellae where P-LHCII binds as an additional antenna to **Photosystem I (PSI)**.
3. **Dephosphorylation**: In low light or PSI-favoring light, the phosphatase **TAP38 / PPH1** removes the phosphate group, returning LHCII to PSII (State 1).
