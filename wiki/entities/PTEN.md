# PTEN (Phosphatase and Tensin Homolog)

**PTEN** (UniProt: [P60484](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P60484)) is a major human tumor suppressor dual-specificity phosphatase that serves as the essential negative regulator of the [[PI3K-Akt-mTOR Signaling Pathway]].

---

## ⚡ Enzymatic Antagonism of PI3K Signaling

```
PI3K (Class IA) ───► Converts PIP2 (4,5) to PIP3 (3,4,5) ───► Activates AKT1
                                 │
                                 ▼
                     PTEN Lipid Phosphatase
       (Dephosphorylates 3-position: PIP3 ──► PIP2)
                                 │
                                 ▼
                      Blocks AKT1 Activation
```

* **Lipid Phosphatase Activity**: PTEN dephosphorylates the D3 position of the inositol ring of phosphatidylinositol-(3,4,5)-trisphosphate ($\text{PIP}_3$), converting it back to phosphatidylinositol-(4,5)-bisphosphate ($\text{PIP}_2$).
* **Loss of Function in TNBC**: Loss of PTEN via genomic deletion, promoter methylation, or inactivating missense mutations removes the primary physiological brake on $\text{PIP}_3$ accumulation, driving constitutive membrane recruitment and hyperactivation of [[AKT1]] and [[PDK1]].
