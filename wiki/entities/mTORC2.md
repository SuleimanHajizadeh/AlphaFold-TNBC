# mTORC2 (Mechanistic Target of Rapamycin Complex 2)

**mTORC2** is a multi-protein kinase complex centered around the serine/threonine protein kinase **mTOR** (mechanistic target of rapamycin), assembled together with **Rictor**, **mSin1**, and **mLST8**.

---

## ⚡ Role in [[AKT1]] Hydrophobic Motif Phosphorylation

```
               PI3K Activation / PIP3
                         │
                         ▼
        mTORC2 Complex (mTOR + Rictor + mSin1)
                         │
                         ▼
             Phosphorylates Ser473
     (AKT1 C-Terminal Hydrophobic Motif - HM)
                         │
                         ▼
           Maximal Catalytic Hyperactivation
```

1. **Hydrophobic Motif Kinase**: While [[PDK1]] phosphorylates Thr308 on the catalytic [[Kinase Activation Loop & Allostery|Activation Loop]], **mTORC2** phosphorylates **Ser473** in the C-terminal hydrophobic motif of [[AKT1]].
2. **Allosteric Stabilization**: Phosphorylation of Ser473 allows the hydrophobic tail to fold back and insert into an allosteric pocket on the small N-terminal lobe of AKT1's kinase domain, stabilizing the active conformation and boosting kinase activity multi-fold.
3. **Differential Drug Sensitivity**: Unlike mTORC1, mTORC2 is acutely insensitive to classic rapamycin treatment, necessitating dual mTOR kinase inhibitors or allosteric AKT targeting in oncology.
