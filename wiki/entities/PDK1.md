# PDK1 (3-Phosphoinositide-Dependent Protein Kinase-1)

**PDK1** (encoded by *PDPK1*, UniProt: [O15530](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/O15530)) is a master serine/threonine kinase belonging to the AGC kinase family. It functions as the primary upstream activator of at least 23 AGC kinases, including [[AKT1]], S6K, SGK, and PKC.

---

## ⚡ Role in [[AKT1]] Activation

```
PIP3 Generation (PI3K)
          │
          ├───────────────────────────────┐
          ▼                               ▼
PDK1 (PH Domain membrane docking)   AKT1 (PH Domain membrane docking)
          │                               │
          └────────► Phosphorylates Thr308 (Activation Loop)
```

1. **Membrane Co-localization**: Both PDK1 and [[AKT1]] contain Pleckstrin Homology (PH) domains that bind membrane $\text{PIP}_3$ generated by PI3K ([[PI3K-Akt-mTOR Signaling Pathway]]). This colocalization brings PDK1 and AKT1 within effective interaction distance.
2. **Activation Loop Phosphorylation**: PDK1 specifically phosphorylates **Thr308** within the [[Kinase Activation Loop & Allostery|Activation Loop]] of AKT1, inducing the active DFG-in state.
3. **Constitutive Basal Activity**: Unlike AKT1, PDK1's kinase domain is constitutively active; its activity towards substrates is primarily controlled by substrate accessibility, phosphorylation status, and membrane recruitment.
