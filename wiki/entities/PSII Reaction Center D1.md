# PSII Reaction Center D1 Protein

The **D1 protein** (encoded by chloroplast gene *psbA*) is one of the two core reaction center proteins (alongside D2/PsbD) of **Photosystem II (PSII)**. It coordinates the primary electron donor P680, pheophytin, the oxygen-evolving manganese-calcium cluster ($\text{Mn}_4\text{CaO}_5$), and the plastoquinone exchange site $Q_B$.

---

## ⚡ Target of [[STN8]] Phosphorylation & Photodamage Repair

Due to the extreme redox potentials generated during water oxidation ($+1.25\text{ V}$), the D1 protein is subject to unavoidable oxidative photo-damage (photoinhibition) under light:

```
Light-Induced Oxidative Damage to D1
                 │
                 ▼
     STN8 Kinase Phosphorylation (Thr-2 on D1)
                 │
                 ▼
Disassembly of PSII-LHCII Supercomplexes & Grana Unstacking
                 │
                 ▼
FtsH Stromal Protease Cleaves & Degrades Damaged D1
                 │
                 ▼
De Novo Synthesis of D1 & Reassembly of Active PSII
```

1. **Specific Substrate of [[STN8]]**: STN8 phosphorylates the N-terminal Thr-2 residue of D1 (along with Thr residues on D2, CP43, and PsbH).
2. **Protective Disassembly Mechanism**: Phosphorylation induces electrostatic repulsion that loosens the tight packing of grana thylakoids, facilitating lateral migration of damaged PSII complexes to the stroma lamellae where the membrane protease **FtsH** can access and degrade damaged D1.
3. **Turnover Cycle**: Newly translated D1 from chloroplast ribosomes is integrated to restore active PSII complexes.
