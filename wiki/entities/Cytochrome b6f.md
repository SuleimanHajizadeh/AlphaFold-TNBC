# Cytochrome b6f Complex

The **Cytochrome $b_6f$ Complex** is a dimeric, multisubunit integral membrane protein complex embedded in the thylakoid membrane of chloroplasts and cyanobacteria. It bridges **Photosystem II (PSII)** and **Photosystem I (PSI)** by transferring electrons from plastoquinol ($\text{PQH}_2$) to plastocyanin (or cytochrome $c_6$) while pumping protons from the chloroplast stroma into the thylakoid lumen (Q-cycle).

---

## ⚡ Role as the Redox Sensor for STN7 Kinase

In addition to its central bioenergetic electron-transport function, Cytochrome $b_6f$ acts as the **primary biochemical sensor** that controls [[Thylakoid State Transitions]]:

```
PSII Reduction ──► Plastoquinol (PQH2) Accumulation
                           │
                           ▼
          Binds to Qo site of Cytochrome b6f
                           │
                           ▼
          Allosteric Activation of STN7 Kinase
                           │
                           ▼
                Phosphorylation of LHCII
```

* **The $Q_o$ Site Sensor**: The occupancy of the quinol oxidation site ($Q_o$ site) of the Cytochrome $b_6f$ complex by reduced plastoquinol ($\text{PQH}_2$) triggers a conformational signal transmitted to the stroma-exposed kinase domain of [[STN7]], switching it into an active catalytic state.
* **Inhibition**: Plastoquinone antagonists (such as DBMIB) that bind to the $Q_o$ site or oxidation of the PQ pool (e.g. under far-red light) prevent STN7 activation.
